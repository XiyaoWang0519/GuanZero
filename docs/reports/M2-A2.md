# Stage A2: learned tribute feasibility

September 21, 2026 (local date). Status: experiment complete; retain heuristic.
This advances M2 engineering without marking its external strength gate met.

## Experimental contract

- Base: the immutable M1 final v1 checkpoint, weight identity
  `8a8e2b08dec2e73998092a67cbc25c26df75b4c0d2f02fd0a791d5d16cd4f745`.
- The acting observation and legal exchange candidates are copied before any
  counterfactual. Each candidate starts from the exact same engine snapshot;
  subsequent exchanges use the heuristic and play uses the frozen FP32 argmax
  policy. Native snapshots stay in memory, and hidden hands are not learner
  features. The target is the original actor's final round return, centered
  over candidates from the same position.
- Natural full-match collection uses separate deal, round-sampling and policy
  RNG streams. All decisions from a match stay in the same train/holdout split.
  Default sampling is 2% of rounds; the bounded offline experiment uses 100%
  and explicitly records that choice. Forced single-candidate exchanges are
  skipped for fitting but retained and counted in evaluation.
- Shared towers, the play head and auxiliary heads stay frozen. Only the two
  exchange fusion heads train with equal weight per sampled position and Huber
  loss. The output checkpoint is marked A2; DMC refuses its different optimizer.
- Evaluation exchanges learned/heuristic team assignments over identical deals
  while enforcing exact equality of all play-model tensors. Independent seeds,
  paired bootstrap intervals, anti-tribute, actual-choice and changed-choice
  counts are reported. Small or inconclusive results cannot promote the heads.

## Early plumbing experiment

256 nontrivial exchange positions from 16 natural matches produced 3,255
counterfactual branches in 13.09 seconds on the local CPU. There were 38 tribute
and 218 back-tribute positions; 229 had different outcomes across candidates.
A 500-update fit changed only the permitted heads. Its 49-position, three-match
holdout had zero mean advantage over the heuristic. This was a plumbing check,
not evidence to replace the heuristic. The original checkpoint was preserved.

## Larger experiment

Collection completed in 205.72 seconds on two local CPU threads: 4,096
positions and 52,257 counterfactual branches from 293 source matches and 3,250
rounds. There were 639 tribute and 3,457 back-tribute positions; 3,724 had
nonconstant candidate returns. Seed 20260925 and 100% round sampling were used
under a 600-second cooperative bound. The shared split contained 235 training
matches (3,268 positions) and 58 held-out matches (828 positions).

The predeclared fits used seeds 23, 24 and 25, 1,000 steps, 32 positions per
minibatch, and learning rate 0.001. Fit times, including loading/caching, were
5.04, 4.92 and 4.41 seconds. Every non-exchange tensor remained exactly equal
to the base model. Each candidate received the same 1,000 fresh duplicate
deals (2,000 rounds) under evaluation seed 20260926.

| Fit seed | Held-out branch advantage over heuristic | Arena net levels/round | Paired bootstrap 95% interval | Deals with changed choices |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 23 | -0.10628 | -0.0815 | [-0.1430, -0.0200] | 722 |
| 24 | -0.13406 | -0.0645 | [-0.1285, +0.0000125] | 705 |
| 25 | -0.14493 | -0.0905 | [-0.1560, -0.0265] | 727 |

**Decision: retain heuristic tribute/back-tribute.** All three means were
negative; seeds 23 and 25 had intervals wholly below zero, while seed 24's
interval included zero. Held-out branch ranking also favored the heuristic
for every seed. No candidate was selected or promoted. These results concern
this base model, data and training budget; they do not establish that learned
tribute can never help.

All candidates saw 101 anti-tribute deals and 899 deals with real choices.
Anti-tribute paired scores were exactly zero, as required by the identical
play models. The arena uses uniformly shuffled decks, levels and previous
finishing orders, not the natural full-match distribution. The three candidates
share the evaluation deals and training data: this is 1,000 unique evaluation
deals reused across seeds, not 3,000 independent deals. Intervals resample
whole paired deals. A future candidate must use a new confirmation set after
selection; these results do not satisfy an external benchmark gate.
