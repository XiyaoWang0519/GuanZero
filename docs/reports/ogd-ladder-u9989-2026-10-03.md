# OpenGuanDan ladder: u9989 against the 1st competition bots

Date: October 3, 2026. Cloud container, CPU only, 3 workers, about 45 minutes.
Raw summary: [ogd-ladder-u9989-2026-10-03.json](ogd-ladder-u9989-2026-10-03.json).

## Setup

- Referee: the OpenGuanDan table (github.com/GameAI-NJUPT/OpenGuanDan, NJUPT,
  the protocol the 1st National Guandan AI competition was played on). Whole
  matches from level 2 until a team passes A.
- Opponents: the 16 competition bots shipped in DanLM's `baselines/`
  (github.com/dashidhy/DanLM, already bug-fixed by DanLM's authors), each in
  its own process behind `eval/ogd_ladder/glue/`. Two protocol differences
  are translated in `bot_host.to_2020`: the four-joker bomb (`FourKings` ->
  `["Bomb", "JOKER", ...]`) and integer ranks in the `beginning` message.
  3rd-ez and part-369 match their chosen cards to `actionList` by exact card
  order, which OpenGuanDan does not preserve; their glue swaps in an
  order-insensitive lookup with the same contract.
- Ours: `u9989.pt` (sha256 07fc81e4...), greedy, no search, heuristic tribute,
  through a `gd` mirror under the `ogd` profile with a fresh history stream
  per round. The policy was trained under the `house` profile.
- 50 seeds per bot, each played twice with the teams swapped: 100 matches,
  about 900 rounds per bot; 1,600 matches and 14,450 rounds in all.

Harness health over the whole run: 0 rounds where the mirror lost the table,
0 finishing-order mismatches, 0 history-stream gaps. 0.4% of our decisions had
a canonical candidate the table does not list (wild readings, known from
RULES.md 13.1); it was dropped from that choice. Bot failures: part-369
crashed 4 times following a four-joker bomb (its own rank list lacks
`JOKER`); those 4 decisions were random legal moves. No other bot failed.

## Reading

- u9989 beats 12 of 16 bots clearly, is roughly even with njupt, 3rd-ez and
  part-369, and loses to 2nd-no-ai.
- Against the five bots DanLM reports, DanLM's single-round win rates are
  80.9-85.9% (README, random levels and tribute, 1000 rounds); ours on this
  table are 49.5% (njupt), 65.2% (chick-squad), 67.2% (guanglan-iot), 68.8%
  (egg-expert) and 63.3% (egg-pancake). The protocols differ (match levels
  here, random levels there), so the comparison is indicative only, but the
  gap matches the direct measurement of about -1.7 levels per round against
  DanLM.
- 2nd-no-ai is the outlier. Its glue computes the move inside the bot's own
  `State` and every chosen move was found in `actionList` in the glue checks,
  so the result is taken as real until a bot-vs-bot calibration says
  otherwise. Not yet checked: no-ai against other bots on this table.
